FSG e-logbook: engineer guide

Every visit
  1. Scan the QR sticker next to the fire panel.
  2. Check the compliance summary and any open faults or disablements.
  3. Tap Engineer access, pick your name and enter your FSG Timesheet PIN (the same PIN you use for timesheets).
  4. Test the call point shown for this week and check every interface listed (lifts, AOVs, door releases, ARC signal).
  5. Record in your JobLogic notes: the call point, interfaces checked, ARC confirmed, and faults before and after.
Codes are shown only after your PIN is accepted, and every view is logged with your name. Never write codes down or share them. The codes in the video are examples only.
